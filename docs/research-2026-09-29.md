## Research caveats

- Variational's live documentation says Q4 2026; an older search snippet said Q3. The live source takes priority. The quarter-end bound is not an exact closing date.
- Pacifica's moved program page gives 4 September without a year. No year was inferred from the weekday.
- Nado's final Season 1 payout on 22 May is distinct from its 21 May season end. Its combined historical total includes other phases, so no Season 1 total was assigned.
- StandX's launch article body says 24 November 2025, while its publication header is a day earlier. The event date is used with a visible caveat.
- Hibachi bonus campaign dates, GRVT article dates, Aster promotion dates and edgeX's old TGE target do not establish full program boundaries.
- Avantis and Reya were not added: this pass did not establish sufficiently clear official program records.
